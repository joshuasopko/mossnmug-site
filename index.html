About

Where the wild sits down and stays a while.

Moss & Mug is nestled inside Juniper Apothecary, where intentional living isn't a trend — it's the whole point. Come for the coffee. Stay for the quiet.

Inside Juniper Apothecary · Sioux Falls, SD

We brew coffee and tea the way nature intended things to go — without rushing, without pretense. Pull up a seat, let the kids wander, let yourself breathe. This is your unhurried corner of Sioux Falls.

"Not everything that's good for you has to feel like medicine."
Philosophy

We believe nature knows what it's doing.

Against the grain

We're not interested in what's mainstream. We're interested in what's true. Real ingredients, real plants, real conversation.

Your body, your call

We trust you to know what you need. We're just here to give you good options and a comfortable chair while you figure it out.

Slow on purpose

There's a reason moss doesn't rush. We built this space to be the antidote to the 30-second drive-through.

Rooted in the wild

From the herbs in your cup to the botanicals on the shelf beside you, everything here traces back to the earth.
