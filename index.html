Not a drive-through. Not a chain. Just good things, done right.

Moss doesn't grow where things move fast. It finds the quiet corners and the shaded rocks, and it thrives there. Slow, deliberate, deeply rooted.

That's the energy we pour into every cup. A proper latte for anyone who wants one. A cortado for the conversation that needs it. No pretense either way.

That's the whole idea.

“The mug is the thing you wrap both hands around on a cold morning. The thing that says slow down, you're not late.”

Our Christmas menu

Winter, brewed.

Housemade syrups, warm spices, and a little Christmas magic.

Lattes & cold brew

Peppermint Mocha

Rich housemade mocha meets cool, real peppermint, made with no artificial flavors. Finished with a peppermint candy for the kind of warmth that feels like coming in from the snow.

Nutcracker Latte*

Toasted pecan, deep caramel, and smooth vanilla, served with a chocolate candied pecan. A little bit of Christmas magic, one sip at a time.

* Contains tree nuts (pecans). Please let us know about any allergies.

Gingerbread Brûlée Specialty

Warm gingerbread spice with a toasted housemade marshmallow on top and dye-free Christmas sprinkles. It tastes like the cookies you baked with someone you love.

Sugar Plum Cold Brew

Smooth cold brew swirled with housemade sugar plum syrup, crowned with a dreamy purple sugar plum cold foam and a sparkle of plum sugar. Like a Christmas Eve fairy tale in a cup.

Warm & snug. For the slow winter days.

Elderberry Remedy

Our housemade elderberry syrup with honey, cinnamon, and warm spices. A comforting cup that feels like being taken care of.

Apple Chai Steamer Contains dairy

Steamed milk infused with our Juniper Chai and sweetened with housemade apple crisp syrup, dusted with cinnamon. Caffeine-free and made for slow winter days. Make it dirty with a shot of espresso.

Apple Cider Hot or iced

Crisp, sweet, and just right. Served warm to hold in your hands or iced for a little bit of fall that lingers.
